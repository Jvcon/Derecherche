Palette workbench

Open the Palette directly with a chosen query and starting category. This page is for iterating on layout, density, keyboard flow, and the recommendation strip.

Try: the extracted keyword starts in the input bar. Press TAB to turn it into a token and enter category mode. With no category entered, typing searches the whole taxonomy; after TABing into a category, typing is scoped to it. TAB again appends the highlighted category, e.g. [←] [keyword] [Category 1 / Category 2]. Enter opens the highlighted engine, and ⌘K/Ctrl+K opens the action menu.
